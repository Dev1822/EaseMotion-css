Noise Grain Glassmorphism Panel

Elevate standard glass cards with a subtle CSS inline SVG noise grain overlay (`feTurbulence`), backdrop blur, and tactile refraction.

✨

Default Glass Panel

Class: .noise-glass-panel

Features frosted backdrop blur (12px) layered with an SVG grain noise texture overlay (`mix-blend-mode: overlay`).

🚀

Interactive Hover Panel

Class: .noise-glass-panel.noise-glass-panel-hover

Hover over this card to experience enhanced blur, heightened refraction border brightness, and smooth 3D elevation.

💎

Glow Accent Panel

Class: .noise-glass-panel.panel-glow

Imbued with a soft vibrant primary glow and polished light edge highlight for featured UI cards or CTAs.

🌙

Dark Glass Variant

Class: .noise-glass-panel.panel-dark

Rich dark translucent background tone ideal for dark mode dashboards, developer tools, and high contrast UIs.

🎨

Tactile High Grain

Class: .noise-glass-panel.noise-high

Presents an elevated noise opacity (0.15) creating a distinct paper-like textured glass aesthetic reminiscent of Linear and Vercel.

⚡

Subtle Grain Panel

Class: .noise-glass-panel.noise-subtle

Ultra-subtle noise opacity (0.04) for minimalist interfaces needing smooth frosted glass blur with zero visual noise clutter.

Quick Usage

Zero dependencies. Self-contained CSS.

<!-- HTML Structure -->
<div class="noise-glass-panel noise-glass-panel-hover panel-glow">
  <div class="panel-header">
    <h3 class="panel-title">Tactile Glass Component</h3>
    <p class="panel-subtitle">Sub-heading text</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel-body">
    <p>Frosted glass blur layered with SVG noise texture.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel-footer">
    <button class="btn">Action</button>
  </div>
</div>
